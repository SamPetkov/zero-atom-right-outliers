Exact zero weights occur naturally in hard-gated covariance estimators and ReLU-derived information matrices, but existing self-coupled outlier theory assumes an invertible diagonal weight matrix. We show that inactive columns may be removed exactly, while their random normalization and the truncated active mark law are retained. This proves that zero weights create the expected zero-spectrum atom but no additional positive right outliers: the original finite-dimensional determinant still gives all separated outliers, with multiplicity and eigenspace information. The result links random matrix theory, high-dimensional statistics, spectral estimation, and neural-network curvature through a concise and broadly reusable mechanism for a broad physics audience.
