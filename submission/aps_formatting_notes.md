# APS / Physical Review Letters formatting notes

Checked 20 July 2026 against current APS author guidance.

- The Letter uses `revtex4-2` with `aps,prl,reprint`.
- PRL Letters have a 3,750-word core limit, corresponding to roughly four journal pages. The current Letter is below that limit.
- The abstract is one paragraph, self-contained, and contains no numbered references or displayed equations.
- The manuscript is written for a broad readership and places the core claim, physical/statistical context, and limitations in the main text.
- Reference titles are included for every reference.
- The main manuscript is a single PDF with its figure in place.
- Supplemental Material is supplied as a separate, self-contained PDF with the manuscript title and author at the top.
- The main manuscript cites the Supplemental Material as a numbered reference and lists all references used by the supplement.
- The data availability statement follows the Acknowledgments and cites the intended public repository.
- The manuscript includes the disclosure required for substantive AI use.
- A cover letter and approximately 100-word editorial justification are supplied.
- PDF metadata identifies the title and author.
- The final public data/software archive should preferably receive a persistent identifier, for example through a GitHub-to-Zenodo release.
