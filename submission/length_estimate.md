# PRL length estimate

The compiled Letter is 3 journal-style pages. APS states that a PRL Letter has a 3,750-word-equivalent core limit and that manuscripts of about four REVTeX PRL pages are likely to comply.

A conservative estimate for this manuscript is below 2,000 word equivalents:

- body prose and caption text after excluding the abstract, acknowledgments, data statement, and references: substantially below 1,000 words;
- displayed mathematics: fewer than 30 single-column rows at 16 word equivalents per row;
- one two-column figure with aspect ratio approximately 1.62, corresponding to about 412 word equivalents under the APS formula.

The exact count used by APS production may differ, but the manuscript has a large margin below the 3,750-word limit.
