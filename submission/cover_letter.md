Please consider the manuscript **“Spectral Outliers of Self-Coupled Covariance Matrices with Exact Zero Weights”** by Samuil Petkov for publication in *Physical Review Letters*.

Self-coupled covariance matrices arise when each sample is weighted by a function of the same low-dimensional coordinates that carry its signal. Existing effective outlier theory for Gaussian-mixture models assumes that the diagonal weights are invertible, excluding hard gates such as the derivative of a rectified linear unit. The manuscript shows that, when the nonzero weights are uniformly separated from zero, inactive columns can be deleted exactly before linearization. After retaining the random active-sample normalization and the exact truncated mark law, the positive right-outlier determinant remains unchanged.

The result gives multiplicity-preserving eigenvalue counts, excludes spurious right outliers, identifies simple-root eigenvector normalization and repeated-root spectral projections, and reconciles the limiting atom at zero with the exact finite-dimensional kernel. The mechanism connects random matrix theory, high-dimensional statistics, spectral estimation, and neural-network curvature.

The manuscript is submitted as a Letter because the active-column principle is concise, broadly reusable, and resolves an explicit obstruction in an existing effective spectral theory. A separate Supplemental Material file contains the complete proof and computational checks.
