\title{Right Outliers of Self-Coupled Sample Covariance Matrices with Exact Zero Weights}

\author{Samuil Petkov}
\email{samuil.petkov@ens.psl.eu}
\affiliation{\'Ecole normale sup\'erieure -- PSL, 45 rue d'Ulm, 75005 Paris, France}

\date{20 July 2026}

\begin{abstract}
Self-coupled sample covariance matrices arise when each sample is weighted by a function of the same fixed-dimensional coordinates that carry its signal. Existing effective outlier theory for Gaussian-mixture models assumes an invertible diagonal weight matrix and therefore excludes hard gates such as the derivative of a rectified linear unit. We prove the nonnegative zero-gap extension: when every weight is either zero or belongs to a fixed interval $[\tau,M]$, exact zeros create the atom $(1-\phi\pi)_+$ at the spectral origin but do not change the positive right-outlier determinant. The proof removes inactive columns before linearization, retains both random active-sample ratios, and applies the source anisotropic weighted-Wishart estimate only to the independent orthogonal Gaussian block, with the exact truncated active variables treated as arbitrary fixed-dimensional marks. We establish multiplicity-preserving eigenvalue counts, absence of spurious right outliers, simple-root eigenvector normalization, repeated-root spectral-projection convergence, and the exact finite-dimensional kernel. The pure half-space gate gives a rigorous ReLU Gauss--Newton block at a fixed non-kink parameter.
\end{abstract}
